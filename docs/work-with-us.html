Each MCP server answers one question from a public system

  • Who manages this land? Is any of it a wilderness study area or a military range?USGS PAD-US
  • Which geothermal leases are held here, and which are producing?BLM MLRS
  • Which transmission rights-of-way cross the area, and which are still pending?BLM MLRS
  • Is any of the site in a flood zone?FEMA NFHL
  • Which listed species, critical habitat, and wetlands are here?USFWS IPaC
  • Where exactly have those species been observed, not just their range?GBIF
  • Which EPA screening flags trip within a mile: Superfund, RCRA, impaired waters?EPA NEPAssist
  • How steep and how well-drained is the ground?USDA NRCS soils
  • Does the area overlap reservation or Tribal trust lands?Census TIGERweb
  • Which historic places are listed nearby?NPS National Register
  • What schedule did energy projects in this state get on the Permitting Dashboard?Permitting Council
  • What do the geothermal leasing rules say, and when did they last change?eCFR
  • Which rulemakings and executive orders changed them, and what did each do?Federal Register

Where do you come in?

Two ways in. Pick yours.

Build what is missing I run a system that energy infrastructure reviews depend on Wrap it as one more server, and every assistant on the protocol can ask it. Contribution guide or Use what is built I have a question about an energy project site Install once, open Claude Code, VS Code, or Codex, and ask. Every answer names its agency. Quick start

Either way, it all speaks the Model Context Protocol, an open standard.

What the toolkit represents today

Every result names its source agency and says where coverage stops.

Public records and land status
BLM case and land-status records for geothermal, mineral, and oil-and-gas activity; federal regulations and rulemakings; EPA brownfields records.
Spatial context
Jurisdictions, land-use plans, protected areas, species and habitat, wetlands and flood hazards, cultural and Tribal context, air quality, nearby communities.
Maps and auditable results
Interactive project-area maps and downloadable spatial data, with source agencies named and gaps flagged.
