# Support

NEPA MCP is open-source research software maintained on a best-effort basis.
The project does not provide a response-time guarantee, production operations
support, or an availability commitment for upstream agency services.

## Bugs

Use the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/pnnl/nepa-mcp/issues)
for reproducible defects in this repository. Include:

- the NEPA MCP version and installation method;
- the operating system and Python version;
- the MCP client and affected server name;
- minimal steps to reproduce the behavior;
- the expected and observed results; and
- sanitized logs or output, when useful.

Do not include credentials, private project data, controlled information, or
suspected vulnerability details in a public issue.

## Usage questions

Usage questions may also be opened in the issue tracker. Check the README, MCP
tool catalog, and geographic-input guidance first. A complete question should
identify the tool, inputs, client, and the point where the documented behavior
was unclear.

## Security reports

Do not report suspected vulnerabilities through public issues. Follow the
private reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and do not
include credentials, controlled information, or sensitive project data in the
initial message.

## Agency, legal, and project determinations

The maintainers cannot provide legal advice, make NEPA or permitting
determinations, speak for an agency or Tribe, or validate a project-specific
compliance conclusion. Confirm material findings against authoritative records
and consult the responsible agency, Tribe, or qualified professional as
appropriate.

## Upstream data and service issues

Many tools depend on public third-party and government services. A source
outage, schema change, rate limit, or incomplete response may be outside this
project's control. Reports are still useful when they include the source URL,
UTC observation time, affected tool, and returned status or error, without
sensitive data.
